# Retained posterior skin contact: opt-in implementation

The initial supine sphere plane came from a 0.25766 m torso inertia radius, leaving the pelvis 0.23815 m above support. `build_supine_surface_contact.py` instead intersects the retained full FJ2810 skin triangles with a 5 mm source-YZ ray grid, selecting the posterior-most source-X intersection per cell. Every quadrature point retains its original skin triangle index, native body attachment, station, and projected reference area. Inner/back skin surfaces cannot duplicate ray area. No mesh vertices are decimated; the explicit integration discretization is the 5 mm projected-area grid.

The first native-ready artifact, `data/derived/supine-surface-contact-exmzq9pq`, has **21,381 points** and **0.534525 m²** projected area. Its bed plane is the full skin surface minimum X, **-0.268803 m**, rather than the inertia-sphere value -0.403499 m. The closest sampled point is 46 micrometers above that full-mesh minimum. Pelvis clearance falls to **0.04177 m**. The current mapped standing/reference pose still puts heels about **0.16 m** above this plane: this contact-geometry repair does not establish an appropriate lying posture.

Attachments use the existing global canonical/native rigid registration and nearest named bone envelope per surface point. The manifest retains registration residuals, source hashes, the original face indices, material priors and all reference stations. Those inferred attachments remain uncertain and are not independently calibrated anatomical joint or skin correspondences. The posterior envelope is a reference-view approximation; large rotations exposing unsampled surfaces need additional surface quadrature. Raster boundary-cell area and between-ray extrema error are explicit limits.

## Constitutive and domain assumptions

The retained skin layers supply thicknesses of 0.1, 1.5 and 5 mm, with the same generic E=3 kPa and Poisson ratio 0.45. The canonical data calls E a source-informed prior, but this implementation has no empirical patient/mattress calibration supporting that value. It is treated as an engineering prior. Layer thicknesses are recovered from their retained volumes divided by full source skin area.

Each quadrature column uses the existing compressible neo-Hookean constitutive form under confined normal compression, with stretch λ=1−δ/h. Since the three layers have the same material constants, series equilibrium gives common λ over their total 6.6 mm thickness. Pressure is `−[μ(λ−1/λ)+Λ log(λ)/λ]`; energy per reference area is `h[μ/2(λ²−1)−μ log(λ)+Λ/2 log(λ)²]`. Force is the negative derivative of stored energy. No force acts in separation. A hard **λ≥0.5** domain prevents excessive compression; this is an engineering validity ceiling, not a validated tissue strain limit. The model is a rigid bed with local skin-column deformation, not a deforming or calibrated mattress.

Native source contact dissipation (2 s/m), equal static/dynamic friction (0.8), viscous friction (0.5) and transition speed (0.2 m/s) are explicitly transferred. Normal force is nonnegative and tangential force opposes slip; damping/friction cannot create energy. This is a deliberate alternative constitutive model, not a claimed equivalent parameterization of the sphere Hertz law.

## Native ownership and verification

`NativeMechanicalStream(surface_contact_manifest=...)` validates and copies the exact source-bound manifest/input. Its native implementation uses **either** the old posterior spheres **or** the retained skin foundation, never both. It shares the same native world plane and adds no inertia. Native calculations cache one transform/velocity per body, cull nonpenetrating body bounds, and skip nonpenetrating points. Point forces are accumulated as body force/torque resultants. The stationary bed receives equal/opposite force and moment; potential energy and power/dissipation are reported.

Source fixtures verify exact-face ray intersection, no inner-surface double counting, projected area, force/potential derivative, force and torque action/reaction, dissipative work, no tension, and compression-domain rejection. The gated `verify_native_surface_foundation.py` compares the old and new contact owners over 2 ms, checks an isolated 1 mm static translation against the Python law, then compares actual native endpoint force, energy and bed moment with the reference evaluator. This is an implementation check, not support acceptance. No candidate or metabolic reference may be promoted before sustained forward support verification.

The contact law's admissible deformation domain is also a feasibility constraint for subsequent statics. The static objective must use generalized force balance with explicit full-body support balance and penetration constraints, rather than allowing reductions in large limb accelerations to reward lifting the body off the bed.

## Selected material-site observations

The optional `surface_sensor_indices` request selects at most 128 unique quadrature row indices without reducing the physical quadrature. `surface_foundation.sensor_points` emits those sites even during separation, permitting sensory release dynamics. Each record includes `id`, `material_identity` (manifest SHA-256, quadrature index, original triangle index), native body owner, source-world rigid reference material point, outward skin normal -X, reaction normal +X, force, projected reference area, actual modeled skin-column `indentation_m`, and a separate `bed_indentation_m` (zero for the present rigid bed). The point is explicitly a reference material attachment, not a reconstructed deformed skin surface. Python/native world coordinates still require the retained rigid transform for canonical consumers. The downstream cutaneous interface may enforce a smaller site cap.

The first native comparison failed because the fixture assumed exactly -1 mm translation while native assembly added -1.73998e-11 m to pelvis_tx. The observed force discrepancy was 3.056e-8 N. Replaying the retained observation at its **actual assembled displacement** reduced force error to 3.79e-14 N and energy error to 1.56e-17 J without changing tolerances or the contact law. Original failure receipt: `data/derived/native-surface-foundation-cats536k`. A final rebuilt native check is required after selected observation support was added.

## Separate measured bed compliance

`ihm/assembly/bed_compression.py` implements the digitized Hong et al. 2022 finite-thickness SM/MM/HM bed curves documented in `BED_MATERIAL_COMPRESSION_EVIDENCE.md`, in series with the unchanged bounded skin columns. It solves equal pressure and `δ_total=δ_skin+δ_bed`, retaining separate compression and stored-energy outputs. The piecewise-linear table, including the soft-foam plateau, is preserved; extrapolation beyond either constituent's retained domain is rejected. The curve is treated as conservative elastic normal response because unloading/rate/hysteresis evidence is absent. This is a source-only candidate; no bed damping or friction calibration is invented.

Source fixture `data/derived/bed-skin-series-ey9az1ng` verifies equal pressure, separate deflections, energy gradient, skin domain and bed non-extrapolation. At the fixed mapped pose, SM supplies at most 512.8 N within its measured range and cannot balance 761.4 N weight. MM can balance normal force after 99.614 mm rigid downward translation: maximum bed deflection is 97.934 mm and maximum skin compression 1.634 mm. HM needs 51.078 mm translation, with bed/skin maxima 48.475/2.557 mm. These are separate local maxima and must not be mistaken for receptor indentation from total body approach.

Normal-force feasibility is not full support equilibrium. The MM center of pressure remains at source Y=1.2067 m, cranial to the COM projection Y=0.9214 m; a substantial unbalanced pitch moment remains. No material choice or translation from this source check is installed as a supported initialization. Subsequent statics must explicitly enforce net force, moment and generalized equilibrium, followed by unchanged forward verification.

## Explicit bed variant and rigid support seed

The optional native `bed_material='MM'` or `'HM'` loads the exact source-bound 200 mm bed curve in series with the skin. MM denotes that specific published specimen scenario, not a generally calibrated mattress. The native series variant preserves separate skin/bed energies, skin indentation, bed indentation and total approach, and rejects either material's domain overrun. Normal response is conservative elastic; the prior rigid-plane normal damping multiplier is explicitly disabled for this new variant because no bed rate law was measured. Existing transferred slip friction remains separately identified. The full quadrature and native human inertia remain unchanged.

A source-only rigid-body support solve varies global pitch, long-axis roll and downward translation, requiring normal force and both contact moments about the COM to vanish. Its search is bounded to 200 evaluations/60 seconds and ±0.2 rad orientations to limit the posterior-envelope approximation. This does not minimize away the support requirement or add an external balancing moment. `build_supported_rigid_seed.py` emits root coordinates but labels them unaccepted until full native generalized/muscle equilibrium and forward support verification.

For MM, `supported-rigid-seed-ljyewh9y` solved these global constraints in 48 evaluations/0.778 s: pitch -0.097897 rad, long-axis roll -0.002738 rad, downward translation 99.728 mm, support 761.3757 N and contact moments about COM below 1.1e-9 N·m. Maximum skin/bed compressions were 1.343/61.983 mm, over 13,398 contacting points. HM, `supported-rigid-seed-pc2jsfs1`, solved in 74 evaluations/0.910 s: pitch -0.110429 rad, translation 73.653 mm, skin/bed maxima 2.476/35.542 mm, over 5,786 contacting points. Native muscle/joint residuals are not part of these source-only checks, and neither seed is installed.

Once a genuinely supported pose is verified, any startup pose must be an immutable identity-bound artifact applied in a fresh native process **before** initial muscle equilibration and immutable metabolic reference sampling. Physical clocks and work/energy accumulators must begin together at zero. There is no authorization to relabel a transient pose as settled or reset M0 inside a continuing run.
