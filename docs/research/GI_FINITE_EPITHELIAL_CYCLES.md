# Finite GI epithelial cycle preparation

`ihm/assembly/gi_epithelial_cycles.py` implements an independent lumen/cell/serosa preparation with five explicit species: Na+, K+, Cl−, neutral glucose and neutral amino acid. It reuses only `R`, `F` and the three-compartment incidence matrix from the skin electrodiffusion primitive. It does not import skin transport coefficients, geometry or voltage priors, and does not accept or mutate native BioGears stores.

The source-conditioned cycle columns are SGLT1 (2 Na + 1 glucose lumen→cell), neutral-AA B0AT1 (1 Na + 1 AA lumen→cell), Na/K pump (3 Na cell→serosa and 2 K serosa→cell), K recycling, nutrient efflux, and separate reversible paracellular Na and Cl paths. References and source applicability are retained in [the primary evidence audit](GI_ELECTROGENIC_TRANSPORT_AUDIT.md). The existing native positive lumped `AminoAcids` is deliberately not mapped to this neutral species.

Every application requests a finite molar extent, caps it jointly by all donors, and applies the same extent to every stoichiometric debit and credit. Floating-point rounding retreats the extent toward zero if needed, rather than clipping individual masses. Zero donors return zero extent. Donor equality is an upper bound; finite chemical affinity generally stops ion/nutrient transfer before a positive donor is exactly exhausted. This preserves each species across all three pools without adding counterions.

Two explicit capacitors join lumen–cell and cell–serosa, with serosa grounded. Fixed initial countercharge establishes the caller's supplied initial potentials. Subsequent ionic transfers change capacitor charge and voltage. No operation restores electroneutrality by inventing chloride or discarding charge. Chemical free energy uses ideal activities at fixed volumes and temperature, with the continuous zero-inventory limit; electrical energy uses the capacitor quadratic form.

An unpowered request stops where its marginal free-energy cost first reaches zero. Convex ideal-mixture and capacitor energy make the directional marginal cost monotone, so a bracketed search takes the last representable lower extent before reversal. Pump requests consume a finite **external** ATP-cycle allowance at a caller-supplied hydrolysis free energy. They fail without changing state if their initial marginal cost exceeds the supplied work per cycle; otherwise their extent stops before marginal cost exceeds it. The endpoint work inequality is checked as well. The difference between supplied work and free-energy increase is recorded as isothermal dissipation. Reverse ATP synthesis is explicitly unsupported. ATP, ADP and phosphate are not explicit chemical species here; this boundary therefore does not claim complete reaction chemistry or predict intracellular ATP concentration. The external allowance must eventually connect once to native energy ownership, not become an additional arbitrary metabolic debit.

This is a finite-cycle accounting fixture, not a time integrator or calibrated kinetic model. The convex marginal-affinity constraint prevents an imposed extent from continuing uphill past equilibrium, but does not specify its time course. Requested extents are interventions, not measured rates. Large requests can be rejected on energy grounds even with adequate donor mass. Test capacitances, volumes and ATP free energies are synthetic priors, not fits to human intestine. No current-density, absorption-rate, portal-delivery or pH prediction is warranted.

Run `.venv/bin/python scripts/verify_gi_epithelial_cycles.py`. Bounded tests cover exact 2:1 transport, donor tails and zero donor use, sodium-limited pump extent, finite ATP exhaustion, rejected unfunded pump and uphill cotransport with unchanged state, reversible downhill paracellular sodium, mixed AA/pump conservation, global charge incidence and work/free-energy/dissipation closure. A scarce-donor synthetic pump case explicitly supplies 200 kJ/mol ATP work to test the donor upper bound; it is not a physiological ATP energy assertion. Other tests use 50 kJ/mol solely as a declared fixture prior.

Next implementation requires separate nutrient/ion activity models, measured intestinal kinetics and geometry, explicit time evolution, and coupled ATP/ADP/Pi and thermal ownership. Preserve this module as a bounded conservative reference while those interfaces are established. The native absorption correction remains unchanged.

Reviewer overshoot regressions were added before the correction. A 0.75 pmol glucose-efflux request from 2 pmol cell to 1 pmol serosa now stops at 0.5 pmol equilibrium. An 8e−17 mol pump request with 1e−11 F capacitors and 50 kJ/mol ATP stops when marginal cycle cost reaches 50 kJ/mol; it no longer accepts a final cost near 74.48 kJ/mol merely because total endpoint energy was affordable.
