# Native GI molar and charge ledger

The retained corrected native absorption method conserves each transported substance, but its gram-based coupling is not transporter stoichiometry. It moves 2 g glucose per 1 g sodium and 1 g lumped amino acids per 1 g sodium. Native molecular weights (180.1559, 22.9898, 89.09 g/mol respectively) imply **3.91817 Na/glucose** and **3.87520 Na/AA pseudomolecule**. The diagnostic checks species mass closure, molar round trips, the zero case, independent sodium in the fat-tail case, and sequential glucose/AA use. It splits source-attributed sodium from the existing independent fluid-associated sodium branch. All cases have zero calcium and chloride credits; this is a fixture limitation, not proof that those species never move elsewhere.

For each case, `F * native_sodium_mol` is the known sodium charge-equivalent transferred into vascular bulk, with the exact negative assigned to lumen. It is uncompensated by a recorded chloride transfer in this method. Global transfer bookkeeping sums to zero; it does **not** demonstrate local epithelial current balance. These coulomb-equivalents are not accumulated physical membrane charge, voltage, current density, or evidence of a pH cause. Those require membrane area, capacitance, time-resolved current paths and chemistry.

The `AminoAcids` substance is a lumped molecule with an alanine-like molecular weight but a renal `ChargeInBlood=Positive` classification. That enum is not a specified numeric valence or composition. Its unknown charge is excluded explicitly, so this is a known-Na ledger, not complete electrical closure. Mapping every amino acid to neutral alanine/B0AT1 would be unjustified.

## Primary measured constraints

* Human SGLT1 expressed in Xenopus oocytes: thermodynamic reversal measurements gave 1.96 ± 0.04 Na per glucose analogue in internally perfused cells above 0.1 mM. Low substrate concentration exposed a sodium leak contribution. This supports a coupled **2 Na:1 glucose** cycle, not attributing all sodium flux to glucose. [Chen et al., 1995](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0006-3495(95)80110-4).
* Mouse B0AT1 expressed in oocytes transported neutral amino acids with approximately **1 Na:1 amino acid**, without obligatory chloride coupling. This is a transporter-specific constraint from mouse, not a human mixed-protein absorption model; other amino acids and transporters need separate representation. [Böhmer et al., 2005](https://doi.org/10.1042/BJ20050083).
* Red-cell ghost measurements support about **3 Na exported per ATP hydrolyzed**. [Garrahan and Glynn, 1967](https://doi.org/10.1113/jphysiol.1967.sp008297). Simultaneous pump-current and sodium-flux measurements in squid axon gave a flux/current ratio 2.87 ± 0.07, consistent with the **3 Na out:2 K in** electrogenic cycle. These establish cycle accounting, not intestinal pump capacity or ATP free energy. [Rakowski et al., 1989](https://doi.org/10.1085/jgp.93.5.903).
* Mouse jejunum Ussing experiments compared wild type and claudin-15 knockout under open and short circuit. Their results support voltage-driven paracellular sodium recycling, so gross apical uptake cannot be identified with net systemic sodium absorption. Glucose did not significantly change measured net chloride flux (14.5 ± 1.8 to 14.0 ± 1.7 µmol/cm²/h; p=0.54, n=5). This directly argues against prescribing an instantaneous equal chloride transfer as the missing mechanism. It does not establish a universal human recycling fraction. [Nakayama et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms21020376).

`data/research/gi_transport/manifest.json` records exact retrieval URLs, UTC date, hashes and license metadata. The 1989 record reports CC BY-NC-SA; the manifest preserves that exact assertion without inventing a version.

## Concrete next native intervention

Reuse the conservative finite-pool/membrane-capacitance primitive being developed for epithelial transport, with independent intestinal topology and measured parameters; do not transfer skin kinetics, area, or permeability. Implement a separate GI module called by the existing no-argument `Gastrointestinal::AbsorbNutrients` during GI PreProcess. Keep stomach digestion, secretion, downstream portal transport and donor caps in their present owners. Introduce explicit lumen, enterocyte and serosal interfaces, rather than treating entry across the apical membrane as immediate vascular delivery.

1. Represent apical SGLT1 cycles in moles: each extent consumes 1 glucose and 2 Na from lumen and credits both to enterocyte. Bound the shared extent by actual donors, including equality and tails. Separate uncoupled sodium transport from SGLT1. Preserve the existing empirical gram-rate only as a declared rate calibration pending human data; changing the stoichiometry changes the implied nutrient maximum, so choose and document which measured capacity is held fixed.
2. Split the lumped amino-acid substrate by charge/transport class before assigning stoichiometry. A neutral-AA B0AT1 branch may use 1 Na:1 neutral substrate as a measured starting constraint, with species and cross-species uncertainty explicit. Do not reinterpret the existing positive enum or apply this rule to all amino acids.
3. Add basolateral Na/K pump cycles: 3 intracellular Na exported, 2 extracellular K imported, 1 ATP hydrolyzed; represent K recycling and basolateral nutrient efflux separately. The diagnostic exposes conditional ATP cycle counts and K recycling requirements, not actual energy expenditure. Native Tissue/Energy must own ATP substrate/heat accounting through one explicit port; adding an extra arbitrary caloric debit here would double count existing metabolism.
4. Add measured, reversible paracellular Na conductance and counterion/exchanger pathways with electrochemical driving forces and a membrane-capacitance/current balance. Do not infer conductance from these bulk fixtures. Fit epithelial area, permeability, intracellular concentrations and open/short-circuit measurements before whole-body acceptance. Na/H and Cl/HCO3 exchange requires tracked H and bicarbonate equivalents; it cannot be simulated as unexplained chloride creation or an enforced charge residual.
5. Expose per-step apical cycle extents, basolateral nutrient fluxes, pump cycles/ATP demand, Na/K/Cl/HCO3 pathway extents, lumen/cell/serosa charge-equivalent residuals, and electrical boundary conditions. Existing bulk store outputs lack these identifiers. Keep gross bidirectional paracellular flux distinct from net portal delivery.

Acceptance requires donor-zero/tail/equality and repeated shared-donor tests; matched 2:1 and conditional 1:1 molar extents; per-species conservation across all three pools; explicit ATP/Na/K pump closure; absence of a chloride source in chloride-free fixtures; reversible paracellular transport; and independently fitted open/short-circuit current and tracer-flux comparisons. The existing 14 cases become bulk conservation regressions, not expected exact parity after a deliberate stoichiometry change. Test zero pump capacity and absent counterion paths to expose finite charge accumulation rather than silently forcing electroneutrality. Integrated glucose and acid-base outcomes remain unproven.
