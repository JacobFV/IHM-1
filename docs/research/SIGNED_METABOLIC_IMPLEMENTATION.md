# Diagnostic signed native muscle port

This implementation follows `SIGNED_METABOLIC_PORT_DESIGN.md` with an explicit restriction: the existing native session has no exact whole-engine rollback. A rejected native step fails the body session closed. It must not produce an accepted exchange, restore only mechanical state and retry, or represent native XML as a complete adapter checkpoint.

The variant builder preserves held source and predecessor libraries. It layers five native objects over the inventoried `whole_body_integrity_shared_donor` parent, which retains the substrate-availability, thermal, humidity and other prior corrections. It copies the exact port header into the variant directory and binds header/source/patch/object/library hashes and compile/link commands in the manifest. The variant refuses to overwrite an existing directory.

The adapter owns an `ihm_signed::Record` and sets its thread-local active pointer with `Scope` across exactly one native 0.02 s step. It validates interval/reference identity and signed chemical/heat/work equality, then checks exactly one Energy and Tissue consumption using `finish`. The adapter must independently enforce consecutive intervals, frozen reference identity, and duplicate sequence rejection. This library does not maintain a hidden history or change native basal/stress writers.

Energy adds `DeltaH` to the native next heat source immediately after the inherited final thermal-source write at the end of Energy PreProcess, and rejects a negative absolute source or simultaneous Exercise. A zero increment skips the scalar setter. Tissue reads the native muscle allocation, obligatory amino-acid availability/O2-limited extent, and mandatory anaerobic allocation before **any Tissue reaction in that call**. The allowed decrement is the remaining discretionary muscle chemical budget after pending AA chemistry. A decrease beyond it throws with requested and allowed watts; no cap, reference adjustment, negative reaction, or transfer from brain/fat is used. The signed increment is added once to muscle demand and once to the total-request check owner. Obligatory AA and mandatory anaerobic requirements retain their native equations. Muscle unmet demand is observed separately.

The native order means Tissue preflight occurs after Energy's thermal solve in a whole-engine step. This is a late domain check, not a transaction. The local fixture calls Energy PreProcess and Tissue metabolism without advancing the whole engine, so it can verify rejection before Tissue mass changes; that does not establish rollback of an integrated step.

Cardiovascular, Endocrine and the two Nervous demand readers use the named effective demand function; Tissue partitioning and all Energy/BloodChemistry writers keep the native base. The four reader observations preserve native phase lag: Cardiovascular sees the incoming base, Endocrine follows current Energy PreProcess, and Nervous sees its normal phase. Zero readers return their original scalar result without extra conversion. Public effective reporting belongs to the adapter.

`verify_signed_muscle_port.py` first compiles small contract fixtures. `--native` compiles a diagnostic executable that loads the frozen XML independently for each case and invokes the actual Energy PreProcess and Tissue metabolism methods. It compares disabled/zero state, positive/negative demand, eccentric heat with identical chemistry, brain/fat independence and the measured native decrement boundary. It retains fixture/executable/state/variant hashes and observations. These checks do not establish absolute native chemical-to-thermal closure: native Tissue's local generated heat remains disconnected from the absolute thermal source, and requested demand is not proof of supplied mechanical work. Substrate-depleted operation requires feedback and additional closure work before acceptance.
