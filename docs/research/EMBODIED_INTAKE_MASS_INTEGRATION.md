# Opt-in embodied consumed-intake endpoint

`EmbodiedRuntime.from_workspace(..., intake_mass=True)` selects the separately
validated `instance_mass_v1` mechanical library. The flag requires an actual
bool and defaults to false. It composes with the existing `regional_skin` and
`source_pin` options. The app actor accepts the same option through its own
factory boundary.

At fresh initialization the factory maps the one canonical stomach centroid
through the retained global registration and inverse native torso transform,
using the exact mapping from `verify_intake_mass_native.py`. It verifies the
plant's retained canonical bytes match the factory's frozen mechanics input.
The generated `intake_mass_binding.json` records canonical entity, source-local
station, canonical and registration hashes, registration prior and the explicit
co-moving ingestion assumption. The factory manifest retains its hash, initial
bridge ownership, selected variant identity, and loaded source receipts for
`intake_mass.py` and `instance_mass.py`. Initial native patient mass already
includes initial GI contents; those contents are not added again.

For each successful 0.02 s exchange, the runtime computes and submits the signed
muscle chemical/heat/work interval exactly once. After native success, unmet
energy and endpoint clock checks, it presents the native consumed-intake receipt
to `IntakeMassBridge`. The bridge can then transfer only the new consumed boundary
at the current mechanical endpoint. The runtime refreshes its mechanical snapshot
before final geometry/frame publication, verifies time and completed muscle
ledger/reference are unchanged, and does not recompute or revise the signed
interval. Mass flux/capture energy remains in the distinct native material ledger.

Initial snapshots, repeated frame reads and scheduled/queued meal acknowledgments
do not apply mass. The frame's `intake_mass` audit reports enablement, binding,
bridge ownership, applied mass and last/pending boundary; disabled frames expose
`{"enabled": false}`. Total explicit payload remains limited to 0.5 kg, with no
implicit splitting, absorption credit or excretion inference.

Any error after physiology was touched aborts both owners. This includes a
consumed-intake validation failure, clock mismatch, capacity rejection, uncertain
mass transfer, or final-frame publication failure. An already consumed native
boundary is not rewound or retried. Mechanical rollback before physiology was
touched retains the runtime's existing behavior. Bridge audit is observational,
not a process restart checkpoint.

`verify_embodied_intake_mass.py` has eight fake-owner tests covering the retained
mass endpoint, unchanged signed interval, no duplicate read credit, uncertain
post-consumption transfer termination without rollback, native clock rejection,
default/strict-bool behavior, exact neutral registration and combined regional
factory source provenance. Existing runtime, regional factory and source-pinned
candidate factory tests also remain part of the focused regression set. These
are integration semantics tests, not acceptance of a live supported whole-body
signed interval. Prior native two-owner consumed mass validation is retained at
`data/derived/audits/intake-mass-bridge-ho3xdh6h`; a factory-only native check, if
run, must be reported separately with zero advances.

## Measured factory acceptance (2026-09-05)

The actual combined `regional_skin=True, intake_mass=True` factory passed in
1.942 s at `data/derived/audits/embodied-intake-mass-factory-7w37j4xm/verification.json`.
It verified both clocks at zero, enabled State mass with zero applied inventory,
zero native consumed count, source and binding hashes, and a scheduled intake
observation that changed neither pending native intake nor mass. Both native
processes were reaped. There were **zero advances**. This receipt establishes
factory composition and cleanup, not a live supported signed-energy interval.
Eight focused fake-owner tests plus eleven existing runtime, three regional
factory, and three candidate factory tests passed. Independent review found no
blocking ordering, ownership or provenance issue within this scope.
