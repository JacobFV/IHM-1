# Canonical anatomical assembly

`data/derived/canonical/anatomy.json` defines one generic adult male body (`ihm-body`). BodyParts3D supplies its 2,234 acquired surfaces and fixes the physical frame: meters, X left, Y superior, Z anterior, origin at the BP full-body bounding-box center. Its skin height is 1.7194712 m. The canonical frame is never normalized or rescaled to another source's nominal person.

Run `.venv/bin/python scripts/build_canonical_anatomy.py`, then `.venv/bin/python scripts/verify_canonical_anatomy.py`. The builder writes isolated anatomy, geometry, lymphatic graph, verification, and application manifest fragment artifacts. `append_to_manifest()` (or `--append`) explicitly integrates the one body family while preserving source families for evidence inspection. Full acquired BP vertices/faces are required; run `scripts/build_spatial_atlas.py` if only previews exist. “Full acquired” means the distributed BP 99% reduction atlas, not an unacquired higher-resolution original.

Five of the 2,234 acquired surfaces are authored twice under two element ids: one celiac artery, two hepatic artery branches, the cricoid cartilage and the hyoid bone. Two rows for one physical structure double its volume, its mass and every link anchored on it, and let a support span a structure to itself. The builder collapses each pair to the lexicographically earlier canonical id, so the assembly holds 2,403 entities over 2,229 source-geometry rows. Identity is decoded-array equality plus an order-independent facet-set hash, not the stored gzip digest, which differs on framing alone; both geometry files stay on disk as the provenance of the collapse and `duplicate_surface_collapse` records every dropped id, its survivor and that hash. See `DUPLICATE-ENTITY-COLLAPSE` in the assumption ledger.

The 23 intervertebral discs carry role `cartilage`, following the record's own FMA is-a anchor FMA55107, a cartilage organ. They had been `rigid_bone`, and therefore infinitely stiff, because BodyParts3D spells the structure "intervertebral disk" while the classifier only matched "intervertebral disc". A disc is the compliance between two vertebrae, so this is the change with dynamical content; the role vocabulary has no fibrocartilage entry, so `cartilage` is nearest, not exact. System stays `skeletal`, as it is for every other cartilage in the atlas, and disc geometry, volume and bounds are untouched. See `DISC-ROLE-FROM-FMA`.

Every entity has a stable canonical ID, source identity, system, physical role, geometry hash, location, bounds, provenance, assumptions, and connections. BP IDs are `body-bp3d-FJ…`; registered Z additions are `body-za-…`. Physical roles distinguish bone, muscle, tendon, ligament, cartilage, nerve, vascular tissue, soft organs, skin, skin layers, regional lymph-node groups, and the lymphatic network. Bounding-box centers are explicitly distinguished from centers of mass. Edge-closed surface integrals supply candidate volumes; open/nonmanifold surfaces have null volume. Edge closure alone does not certify nonintersecting solids.

Z-Anatomy contributes all five missing lung parenchymal lobes, the thyroid gland, lymph-node groups, tonsils, and parathyroid surfaces. The acquired BP lung ontology resolves into airway and vascular constituents; those are preserved but do not substitute for parenchymal lobe surfaces. Shared named bone bounding-box centers constrain a global affine fit plus a regularized thin-plate-spline residual field. The builder records every correspondence, transform, regularization prior, five-fold held-out residual, and local Jacobian determinant over all imported vertices. Shared organs already represented by BP (including spleen and thymus lobes) are excluded. Bone and other competing organ geometries are not stacked into the canonical body. The acquired Z original/evaluated geometry and hashes remain intact. Z is partly derived from BP and is explicitly not an independent human sample.

The first build used 99 bone anchors with a held-out RMS correspondence residual of about 4.67 mm. This measures inter-atlas geometric fit, not biological variability, measurement accuracy, or confidence in an unobserved organ boundary. Positive sampled Jacobians check local folding at evaluated points; they do not certify global injectivity or collision-free interfaces. Source meshes can still overlap.

The published lymphatic graph contributes all 996 vertices, 1,117 structural edges, and 272 source node flags. Its T-pose arms are articulated onto BP arm supports, and its body extent is aligned to the canonical skin. Unlike the named-bone registration, this graph has **no labeled anatomical landmarks**: shoulder/elbow/wrist controls and transverse/depth alignment are recorded modeling priors. Canonical and original positions and lengths coexist. Nearest registered node-group associations within 60 mm are regional candidates, not claims that publisher nodes are the same anatomical nodes. No duplicate node organs are created. Edge direction, lumen radius, lymph flow and valve state remain unknown. The graph provides source topology for subsequent transport models, not solved drainage or a confirmed vessel surface.

The acquired skin supports three nonoverlapping shell integration layers: epidermis, dermis and hypodermis. Their respective thickness priors are 0.1, 1.5 and 5 mm; the recorded modeling ranges are 0.05–0.2, 0.5–3 and 1–20 mm. These are explicitly selected engineering assumptions, not literature measurements or fitted confidence intervals. Each layer references the actual skin surface and an inward depth interval. No offset surface is fabricated or claimed collision-free. Layer volume is the area of the inferred exterior component times the assumed thickness: 1.7804602548390722 m2, supplied by `ihm.assembly.skin_layers.physical_skin_support` against exact geometry bytes and recorded on each layer as `physical_surface_support`. It is not the 3.502598974493317 m2 raw triangle area of the authored slab, which is a two-sided quantity and stays on the skin parent as descriptive source metadata. Extruding against the raw area invented 11.366 L of skin and put 35.85% of body mass in three layers; against the exterior component the same 0.1/1.5/5.0 mm priors give 22.17%, and the 1.6 mm epidermis-plus-dermis stack weighs 3.13 kg against the ICRP reference 3.3 kg. The watertight envelope, 1.7812538727 m2, agrees to 0.045% and is a cross-check, not the divisor: it seals 36 apertures with 438 synthetic cap faces that are not integument. The skin parent is a boundary support; mechanics must avoid counting both its mass and its layer masses.

Connections labeled `regional_spatial_support` use the nearest bone bounding-box center. They form a usable spatial substrate and are explicitly not measured insertion sites, contacts, articulations, or physiological pathways. Mechanics and physiology add their own specific connections with appropriate source/assumption lineage.

Uncertainty has separate biological, registration/synthesis, and display/numerical records. No confidence percentage is invented. Every added geometry has a source dependency and an assumption-ledger reference. Materialization preserves source surfaces, registered surfaces, shell integration layers and graph chords as distinct representations; they belong to one body but carry different evidence.

Verification checks unique IDs, all raw/reference byte hashes, canonical units, domain roles, connection targets, inference lineage, landmark holdout error, sampled positive Jacobians, laterality, vertical envelope, and exact preservation of lymphatic graph topology. These are source-fidelity and numerical/anatomical consistency checks. They do not establish patient calibration, population variability, empirical predictive accuracy, or a clinically complete digital twin.

The workbench opens `ihm-body` in its core organs/skeleton view. Other layers remain selectable; original source families are under the collapsed advanced evidence control. The inspector presents biological, registration/synthesis, and numerical/display uncertainty separately before the original-source metadata. Display opacity is a visual control, not a confidence weight.

Computed playback consumes `/api/body/trajectory` and reference `centroids_m`, applying `T(c + translation) R F T(-c)` to each tissue's acquired geometry. Missing entity transforms reset to identity at that frame. Invalid, absent, or empty trajectories leave the anatomy static with playback disabled. The temporal panel's generic-body option uses physiology from those same computed frames; source trajectories remain explicitly selectable. Canonical scenario submission uses `/api/body/scenarios` and the fixed `IHMGenericMale` profile. Completed canonical runs load their associated body trajectory. Recorded sample timestamps control playback; no interpolated motion or extrapolated physiology is manufactured.
