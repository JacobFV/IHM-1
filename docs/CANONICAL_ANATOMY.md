# Canonical anatomical assembly

`data/derived/canonical/anatomy.json` defines one generic adult male body (`ihm-body`). BodyParts3D supplies its 2,234 acquired surfaces and fixes the physical frame: meters, X left, Y superior, Z anterior, origin at the BP full-body bounding-box center. Its skin height is 1.7194712 m. The canonical frame is never normalized or rescaled to another source's nominal person.

Run `.venv/bin/python scripts/build_canonical_anatomy.py`, then `.venv/bin/python scripts/verify_canonical_anatomy.py`. The builder writes isolated anatomy, geometry, lymphatic graph, verification, and application manifest fragment artifacts. `append_to_manifest()` (or `--append`) explicitly integrates the one body family while preserving source families for evidence inspection. Full acquired BP vertices/faces are required; run `scripts/build_spatial_atlas.py` if only previews exist. “Full acquired” means the distributed BP 99% reduction atlas, not an unacquired higher-resolution original.

Every entity has a stable canonical ID, source identity, system, physical role, geometry hash, location, bounds, provenance, assumptions, and connections. BP IDs are `body-bp3d-FJ…`; registered Z additions are `body-za-…`. Physical roles distinguish bone, muscle, tendon, ligament, cartilage, nerve, vascular tissue, soft organs, skin, skin layers, regional lymph-node groups, and the lymphatic network. Bounding-box centers are explicitly distinguished from centers of mass. Edge-closed surface integrals supply candidate volumes; open/nonmanifold surfaces have null volume. Edge closure alone does not certify nonintersecting solids.

Z-Anatomy contributes missing lymph-node groups, tonsils, and parathyroid surfaces. Shared named bone bounding-box centers constrain a global affine fit plus a regularized thin-plate-spline residual field. The builder records every correspondence, transform, regularization prior, five-fold held-out residual, and local Jacobian determinant over all imported vertices. Shared organs already represented by BP (including spleen and thymus lobes) are excluded. Bone and other competing organ geometries are not stacked into the canonical body. The acquired Z original/evaluated geometry and hashes remain intact. Z is partly derived from BP and is explicitly not an independent human sample.

The first build used 99 bone anchors with a held-out RMS correspondence residual of about 4.67 mm. This measures inter-atlas geometric fit, not biological variability, measurement accuracy, or confidence in an unobserved organ boundary. Positive sampled Jacobians check local folding at evaluated points; they do not certify global injectivity or collision-free interfaces. Source meshes can still overlap.

The published lymphatic graph contributes all 996 vertices, 1,117 structural edges, and 272 source node flags. Its T-pose arms are articulated onto BP arm supports, and its body extent is aligned to the canonical skin. Unlike the named-bone registration, this graph has **no labeled anatomical landmarks**: shoulder/elbow/wrist controls and transverse/depth alignment are recorded modeling priors. Canonical and original positions and lengths coexist. Nearest registered node-group associations within 60 mm are regional candidates, not claims that publisher nodes are the same anatomical nodes. No duplicate node organs are created. Edge direction, lumen radius, lymph flow and valve state remain unknown. The graph provides source topology for subsequent transport models, not solved drainage or a confirmed vessel surface.

The acquired skin supports three nonoverlapping shell integration layers: epidermis, dermis and hypodermis. Their respective thickness priors are 0.1, 1.5 and 5 mm; the recorded modeling ranges are 0.05–0.2, 0.5–3 and 1–20 mm. These are explicitly selected engineering assumptions, not literature measurements or fitted confidence intervals. Each layer references the actual skin surface and an inward depth interval. No offset surface is fabricated or claimed collision-free. Layer volume is surface area times assumed thickness. The skin parent is a boundary support; mechanics must avoid counting both its mass and its layer masses.

Connections labeled `regional_spatial_support` use the nearest bone bounding-box center. They form a usable spatial substrate and are explicitly not measured insertion sites, contacts, articulations, or physiological pathways. Mechanics and physiology add their own specific connections with appropriate source/assumption lineage.

Uncertainty has separate biological, registration/synthesis, and display/numerical records. No confidence percentage is invented. Every added geometry has a source dependency and an assumption-ledger reference. Materialization preserves source surfaces, registered surfaces, shell integration layers and graph chords as distinct representations; they belong to one body but carry different evidence.

Verification checks unique IDs, all raw/reference byte hashes, canonical units, domain roles, connection targets, inference lineage, landmark holdout error, sampled positive Jacobians, laterality, vertical envelope, and exact preservation of lymphatic graph topology. These are source-fidelity and numerical/anatomical consistency checks. They do not establish patient calibration, population variability, empirical predictive accuracy, or a clinically complete digital twin.
